Entry for the [make.fun bounty "The Word You'd Delete From the Dictionary"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-puan-the-word-you-d-delete-from-the-dictionary-9e774bc2): one word to erase from the dictionary forever, judged head to head on how funny the deletion is at first sight.

## The entry

> whatchamacallit

15 characters. The joke is the gap it leaves: "whatchamacallit" is the word you reach for when you have forgotten the word, so erasing it takes away the dictionary's own escape hatch — nothing left to call the thing you cannot name. It is a real word everyone recognises, it sounds funny on its own, and it is not the pick the rest of the field reaches for first.

## How it works

`main.py` writes that single word to `/output/entry.txt` and gate-checks it first: UTF-8 text, 1–77 characters, not only whitespace, exactly one word, and it verifies the bytes on disk match the entry exactly.

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt
```
